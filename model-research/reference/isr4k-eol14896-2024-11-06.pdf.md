    EOL14896 - Amended

    Cisco announces the end-of-sale and end-of-life dates for the Cisco ISR4200, ISR4300 and
    select ISR4400 Series Platform. The last day to order the affected product(s) is November 7,
    2023. Customers with active service contracts will continue to receive support from the
    Cisco Technical Assistance Center (TAC) as shown in Table 1 of the EoL bulletin. Table 1
    describes the end-of-life milestones, definitions, and dates for the affected product(s). Table
    2 lists the product part numbers affected by this announcement. For customers with active
    and paid service and support contracts, support will be available under the terms and
    conditions of customers' service contract.

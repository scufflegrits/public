Capabilities of VMware Cloud Foundation
Operations
VCF Operations delivers centralized, AI-powered visibility and control across
your private cloud to continuously optimize infrastructure performance,
capacity, and cost.
VCF Operations orchestrator is a workflow engine designed to simplify the
automation of complex IT tasks with the ability to build and trigger
orchestrator workflows.
VCF Operations workload mobility streamlines workload mobility and
disaster recovery by providing seamless, secure application migration across
distributed VCF environments without requiring application downtime.
VCF Operations for networks offers end-to-end network visibility and
analytics to optimize traffic flow, troubleshoot connectivity issues, and
confidently plan micro-segmentation security policies.
VCF Operations fleet management: Enables centralized administration,
configuration, and lifecycle management of operational components across
highly distributed Software-Defined Data Center (SDDC), multi-site
deployments.
VCF Operations collector acts as a secure, distributed data-gathering node
that continuously ingests metrics and telemetry from local infrastructure
endpoints into the central operations analytics engine.

The Power of
Unified Infrastructure

As organizations increasingly rely on virtual
machines (VMs) and stateful workloads within
Kubernetes, the need for reliable and resilient
data security has never been more critical.
This is especially true in modern virtualization,
where VMs run alongside containerized
applications. Red Hat OpenShift Virtualization
is paving the way for organizations to manage
VMs and containerized workloads on a
unified platform.

With Veeam Kasten’s powerful data
protection capabilities, organizations that
leverage OpenShift’s platform will benefit
from simplified and secure migrations,
powerful transform capabilities, friction-less
operations, and seamless scalability — all
designed to provide value across a hybrid
environment.

Veeam Kasten facilitates the modernization
of cloud-native applications and VMs in any
OpenShift environment by bridging the gap
between traditional operational models and
modern infrastructure success. This combined
solution fosters collaboration between
development and operations teams to enable
comprehensive data protection strategies
that encompass backup and disaster
recovery (DR) while enabling cross-cluster
application mobility and cyber resilience.

Optimized Virtualization
for OpenShift

Veeam Kasten elevates the OpenShift
experience by unifying data protection
across both VMs and containers. Key
innovations include:

Granular file-level recovery:
Kasten’s file-level recovery for KubeVirt (the
upstream project for OpenShift Virtualization)
VMs enables rapid restoration of critical data
without the overhead that comes with full VM
recoveries. Customers can minimize downtime
and maintain operational continuity during
disruptions to maximize their agility
on OpenShift.

Streamlined VM management:
The Kasten VM dashboard delivers clear,
cross-cluster visibility into KubeVirt VM
protection, empowering OpenShift
administrators to manage their environments
with ease. This translates into reduced
complexity and faster issue resolution,
which keeps systems running efficiently.

One solution for multiple workloads:
Veeam Kasten natively supports both
containerized applications and KubeVirt-
based VMs, enabling platform teams to
standardize data protection across diverse
OpenShift workloads. By consolidating
backup operations through a single
control pane, organizations eliminate
siloed, architecture-specific tools and
reduce operational overhead. This
streamlines policy management and
automation while driving OPEX efficiency
across hybrid environments.

Strengthened Security For Data Resilience

Operating on OpenShift means that safeguarding critical workloads is paramount. Veeam Kasten enhances
security and builds trust with:

Secure, self-service data operations

Empowering namespace owners via granular role-based
access control (RBAC) to initiate secure cross-cluster
migrations and restorations ensures that data movement
remains controlled and compliant. This self-service
capability supports DR and day-to-day operations, which
reduces dependency on centralized IT teams.

Proven commitment to data security
Veeam Kasten is ISO 27001 certified and FIPS 140-3
compliant on OpenShift, ensuring enterprise data
security aligned with federal standards. With support
for immutable backups and availability on Iron Bank,
Kasten helps organizations meet internal policies,
regulatory mandates, and mission-critical availability
requirements with confidence.

Effortless and Scalable Operations

Veeam Kasten’s enhanced automation and intuitive user experience mean that managing data resilience on
OpenShift is simple, efficient, and scalable via:

An intuitive UI
The Veeam Kasten UI focuses on managing policies,
tracking restore points, and monitoring protection
statuses. This clarity reduces the DevSecOps learning
curve, accelerates incident resolution, and simplifies
governance across diverse OpenShift deployments.

Automation for faster recovery
Seamless integration into CI/CD pipelines using GitOps
principles ensures that recovery processes meet rigorous
recovery point objective and recovery time objective (RPO
and RTO) targets. Automated DR workflows mean that
operational interruptions are minimized, which keeps
business momentum intact.

Enhanced Flexibility and Integration

Veeam Kasten’s commitment to supporting a wide range of architectures and storage options delivers critical
value for OpenShift customers, including:

Diverse CPU architecture support

Now extending beyond x86 to include ARM and IBM
Power architectures, Veeam Kasten provides flexibility
in choosing the right hardware to meet your
operational needs. This diversity drives cost efficiencies
and performance optimizations, thus enabling
customers to tailor OpenShift environments to align
with their workloads, whether it’s in the datacenter,
public cloud, or at the network edge.

Advanced backup and storage options
With comprehensive backup integrations, including
support for next-generation storage solutions like
NetApp Trident ONTAP NAS-economy, customers
benefit from having robust offsite backup capabilities.
This secure, integrated storage strategy reinforces
business continuity and data integrity, which is essential
for modern OpenShift deployments. Additionally,
Kasten supports block volumeMode backup, which
is important for KubeVirt workload performance.

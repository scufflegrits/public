Subscription FAQ

## What to know before purchasing

## How many subscriptions do I need?

A subscription is required for each Proxmox Backup Server. Each subscribed server includes unlimited backup storage and unlimited backup clients.

## Are Proxmox Backup Server features limited by subscription level?

No. Incremental backup, deduplication, encryption, verification, S3-compatible object storage, remote synchronization, tape backup, granular recovery, and all other Proxmox Backup Server features are available at every level. Plans differ by technical support entitlement, ticket allowance, and response time.

## What does “response time” mean?

It is the guaranteed first response time for a critical support request (Severity 1) submitted through the Proxmox Customer Portal. Coverage applies during the support hours for the selected plan; for Premium, this will be 24/7.

## Can my organization purchase without using the Online Shop?

Contact sales at sales@proxmox.com to discuss your purchasing requirements. Qualified Proxmox resellers can also provide local purchasing, consulting, implementation, and support services.

## What support is included with Community?

Community includes access to the Enterprise Repository but not ticket support from the Proxmox enterprise support team. Assistance is available through the public Proxmox community forum.

## What are the enterprise support business hours?

Premium includes 24/7 ticket support starting October 19, 2026. Standard will also move to 24/7 ticket support by the end of 2026. Starting October 19, 2026, customers in North America receive business-hours support during North American business hours, while customers in APAC receive business-hours support during Eastern Australian business hours. For customers in other regions, business-hours support remains available Monday through Friday, 07:00–17:00 CET/CEST on Austrian business days. Qualified resellers may offer support in other time zones and local languages. Please contact sales@proxmox.com if you have requirements beyond the standard offerings.
